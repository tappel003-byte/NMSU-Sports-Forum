# Aggie Ring of Honor

**NMSU Sports Forum — Community Memorial**

> *Forever an Aggie. Forever remembered.*

## Purpose

The Aggie Ring of Honor preserves the memory of longtime members of the Aggie Alert community and NMSU Sports Forum who have passed away. Their place here is earned by belonging to the community, not by post counts, popularity, or badges.

**Honor members under the Aggie Alert moniker by which the community knew them.** Real names are not required and should not be sought or published by default. Members who never registered at NMSU Sports Forum may still be honored.

This is a permanent, respectful memorial space, separate from reputation, achievement badges, rankings, or competition.

## Nomination and publication process

1. A member suggests an Aggie Alert moniker privately to an administrator.
2. An administrator verifies the death through reliable, respectful means; never treat absence from the forum as evidence.
3. An administrator considers any known family wishes and whether the entry or its content could intrude on privacy.
4. The administrator prepares a short tribute focused on the person's community presence and submits it for appropriate review.
5. Only after approval is the memorial published to the Ring of Honor.
6. Members can submit additional memories; moderators can review, edit for safety, or remove unsuitable material.
7. Administrators must provide a way to correct mistakes, address family concerns, or remove a memorial when appropriate.

## Site behavior and design

- **Navigation:** a dedicated “Ring of Honor” destination under NMSU Sports Forum, accessible from mobile and desktop.
- **Index:** a dignified, searchable directory of monikers, without popularity sorting or engagement scores.
- **Memorial page:** moniker prominently displayed, optional historical avatar, remembrance, community memories, and permitted legacy links.
- **Reactions:** no thumbs-up/down scoring, leaderboards, or reputation points on memorial entries.
- **Preservation:** memorial pages and tributes are intended to remain accessible permanently, subject to correction, consent, safety, and removal requests.
- **Account protection:** a deceased member's account must be retired/secured and cannot be used to publish new posts.
- **Historical content:** links to archived posts may be shown; do not silently override existing image deletion or content policies. Memorial images must have a separate approved retention policy.
- **Privacy:** no requirement to reveal a person's offline identity. Do not expose private correspondence or unpublished contact information.
- **Moderation:** respectful tributes only; remove harassment, speculation about death, and invasive personal details.
