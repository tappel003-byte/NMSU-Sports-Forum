# Administrator guide

Community administrators run the forum day to day. You don't need any technical access, and nothing needs doing every day.

## Opening the forum
**Admin → Settings → Who can join and read:**
- **Sign-up:** *Invitation only* or *Open*. The plan is invitation-only for the first few weeks, then open.
- **Anyone can read without an account:** visitors can browse discussions, games and recruiting; they must join to post.
- **Troll flags needed to hide a post** (default 3).

## Troll flags and moderator review
Moderators are the forum's administrators.
- **Flags:** registered members tap **Troll** on a post (once per post). When **three different members** flag it, the post is **temporarily hidden pending review**. That is a precaution, not a verdict: the author isn't penalized and **flags never change Aggie Reputation**. A moderator's flag counts as one flag. All moderators get a notification.
- **Review (Admin → Reports → Flagged posts):** you see the post, who flagged it, and each flagger's history (how often their flags were dismissed, and whether they keep flagging the same person). Type a reason, then **Restore** or **Remove**, optionally ticking **Also send a warning**. The member sees the reason.
- **Warnings:** Admin → Members → **Warn**.
- **Suspensions:** one moderator can suspend for **1–90 days**, with a reason. It lifts itself.
- **Permanent bans need three moderators.** Admin → Members → **Propose ban** (your proposal counts as one approval); two more moderators approve under **Admin → Bans & appeals**. Proposals expire after 14 days. With fewer than three moderators, use timed suspensions.
- **Appeals:** members appeal removals, warnings, suspensions and bans (from Settings, or from the sign-in page if suspended or banned). The moderator who made the decision can't decide the appeal; for a ban, none of the approving moderators can. Granting an appeal undoes the decision.
- **Abusive flagging:** **Admin → Bans & appeals → Flagging history** shows each member's flags, upheld vs. dismissed. You can turn off a member's flagging (with a reason); they can still use Report.
- **Audit history:** every decision and its reason is in **Admin → Log**, visible only to moderators.

## Inviting members
**Admin → Invitations → Create invitation.** Optionally enter the person's email (then only that address can use it) and a private note so you remember who it's for. Copy the link and send it by text or email. Links work once and expire after 14 days by default; revoke unused ones any time.

## Helping someone who forgot their password
**Admin → Members → Reset link**, then send them the link. It works once and expires in 3 days. (If the forum has email turned on, members can also use "Forgot your password?" themselves.)

## Moderating
- Open any discussion and tap **Moderate** to pin, lock, move to another forum, remove, or reopen an archived thread.
- Remove an individual post with its trash icon. Removed posts show as "This post was removed."
- In live chat, remove a message with its trash icon; it disappears for everyone immediately.
- **Admin → Reports** lists what members flagged. Resolve or dismiss each one with an optional private note.
- **Admin → Members**: suspend (signs them out immediately) or reinstate, and add or remove administrators. The forum always keeps at least one active administrator.
- Every moderation action is recorded in **Admin → Log**, visible only to administrators.

Private messages are strictly private. There is no way for administrators to read them. If a member reports a message, only that one message is shared with you.

## New-member approval (optional)
Turn on **Admin → Settings → Approve each new member's first post** if you want to check newcomers. Their first post waits in **Admin → Approvals**; once you approve one post, the rest appear immediately. Off by default since the forum is invite-only.

## Timed suspensions and merging
When suspending, enter a number of days and the suspension lifts itself. To combine duplicate threads, open the duplicate → **Moderate** → **Merge into another discussion**.

## Recognition
**Admin → Members → Recognize** (or "Award recognition" on a profile). Pick the recognition and write a short reason; it appears on their profile and in the community contribution history. Recognition never grants moderation powers. Founding Member is automatic, and marking a suggestion **Implemented** awards Idea Contributor automatically.

## Suggestions
In **Suggestions & Improvements**, open a suggestion → **Moderate** → set *Under consideration*, *Planned*, *Implemented* or *Not planned*.

## Sports data
Schedules, results and game threads are automatic (see `SPORTS_DATA.md`). **Admin → Sports data** shows each feed's status. If a feed breaks, administrators get a notification and the forum keeps the last good schedule. **Correct…** on a game is for emergencies only (for example, the official feed has the wrong time). Clear the correction once the feed is right again.

## Settings
**Admin → Settings**: the official launch date (starts the 30-day Founding Member window), the optional Honorary Founding Member threshold, the live chat window size, and a home-page announcement.

## What's intentionally not on the site
